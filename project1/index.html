"A Wishbone Never Breaks Even..."

Wishbone was released on August 15, 2025 through Republic Records as Conan Gray's 4th studio album. The album explores the complexities of love, loss, heartbreak, and every emotion in between. The wishbone acts as a symbol for how the end of relationship - whether romantic, platonic, or familial - often affects one person more intensely than the other. A wishbone never breaks even, and in this case, Conan got the short end of the stick.
